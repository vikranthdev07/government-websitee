HELP CENTRE

Common questions

Is this an official government website?

No. Yojana Setu is an internship demonstration project. Verify scheme information using the relevant official government website.

Does a sample match mean I am eligible?

No. The results use simplified sample rules. Actual eligibility depends on the current official guidelines and your circumstances.

Do I need to enter personal information?

No identity numbers, passwords or bank details are needed for this demo.

Is application tracking connected to a backend?

Not yet. The tracker currently uses fixed demonstration references. A real backend will be a separate development step.

Yojana Setu is a student demonstration project, not an official government website.

Always confirm scheme details and application status with the relevant official authority.
